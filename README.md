# L U M I È R E
Lumière is a modernized VLC media player with a beautiful, Netflix-style interface, achievements, and the ability to stream your local videos to any device in your house over Wi-Fi—no server required.

---

## The Origin

**Lumière** was born from six case studies on piracy.

The research revealed a paradox: the media and film industries spent billions fighting piracy, yet failed spectacularly. Lawsuits, DRM, region-locking—none of it worked. It only made the problem worse.

But one company stood apart.

**Valve and Steam** didn't fight piracy—they made it irrelevant. By offering a legitimate experience that was beautiful, simple, convenient, and owned by the user, Steam turned gamers into customers. Not through force. Through respect.

**The question became:** *"Why not take a page out of Steam's book—or a whole chapter—and apply it to video?"*

---

## The Problem

By 2026, streaming is broken:

- **10+ subscriptions** to watch everything
- **Rising prices** with no end in sight
- **Content disappearing** from platforms you already paid for
- **Ads** in your "premium" tiers
- **Zero ownership**—you're renting everything

Consumers are trapped. Pay more, get less, own nothing.

---

## The Solution

Lumière is a **sovereign media ecosystem** built on the same principles that made Steam successful:

**1. Beauty**
A stunning, Netflix-level interface that makes your library feel premium.

**2. Simplicity**
Double-click to install. Point it at your media. Start watching. No server required.

**3. Ownership**
Your files are your files. Your data is your data. We don't touch either.

**4. Gamification**
Achievements turn watching into a social, engaging experience.

**5. Freedom**
Open source. Free forever. No subscriptions. No tracking. No corporate strings.

It's a tool for people who:
- Buy movies and TV shows on Blu-ray, digital storefronts, or physical media
- Have a collection of files they want to organize and enjoy
- Want to watch their media anywhere in the world (via self-hosted server)
- Want a beautiful, Netflix-like experience for their own library

**Lumière does not provide content.** It organizes and plays what you already own.

**Piracy is a symptom of a broken system.** Lumière is an alternative—a way to actually own and enjoy your media without corporate strings attached.
