# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 28, 2025

---

## 1. Our Promise

**Lumière collects zero personal data by default.**

We believe that your media habits, your library, and your personal information belong to **you**—not to us, not to advertisers, not to corporations.

This Privacy Policy explains exactly what data Lumière collects, what it does not collect, and your rights.

---

## 2. What Lumière Does NOT Collect

Lumière does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

| Category | Data |
|----------|------|
| **Personal Information** | Name, email, address, phone number |
| **Media Content** | What you watch, when you watch it, or your viewing history |
| **Library Metadata** | Your file names, folder structure, or media tags |
| **Location Data** | Your IP address, GPS coordinates, or network information |
| **Device Information** | Hardware specs, operating system, or system settings |
| **Analytics/Telemetry** | Usage patterns, crash reports, or performance metrics |

**All of your data stays on your devices or your own server.**

---

## 3. The Exception: Bug Reports

Lumière includes an optional **"Report a Bug"** feature.

When you voluntarily submit a bug report, you choose what information to include. The report may contain:

- A description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the bug
- System information (optional)

**This data is sent directly to a private Google Form that we control.** It is used solely for debugging and improving the software.

- We do **not** associate bug reports with any personal identifier.
- We do **not** share bug reports with third parties.
- We do **not** use bug reports for marketing or analytics.

**You are in complete control:** You choose what to include in a bug report, and you choose whether to submit one at all.

---

## 4. The Lumière Server (Self-Hosted)

If you choose to run a Lumière Server, **you host the data yourself.**

The server stores:

- User profiles (name, avatar, password—if you choose to set one)
- Achievements
- Watch history
- Friend connections

**All of this data lives on your hardware.** We have no access to it.

**Responsibility:** You are responsible for securing your server, managing backups, and protecting your users' data.

---

## 5. Social Features (Friend Connections)

Lumière's social features are **decentralized** and **peer-to-peer:**

- Friend connections are established **only** between your server and your friends' servers.
- Achievement data is shared **only** with the friends you connect to.
- Recommendations are sent **only** between connected friends.

**We do not host or relay any social data.**

---

## 6. Download Tracking (Abacus API)

We use an external service (**Abacus API**) to count downloads. This gives us a rough estimate of how many people are using Lumière.

**The Abacus API:**

- Increments a counter when a download button is clicked.
- Does **not** collect any personal data.
- Does **not** track who downloaded the file.
- Does **not** set cookies or track across visits.

**This tells us:** *"X number of people downloaded Lumière."*

**This does not tell us:** *"Who downloaded Lumière"* or *"What they do with it."*

---

## 7. Third-Party Services

Lumière may connect to the following third-party services for specific functions:

| Service | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **GitHub API** | Check for updates | None (public API endpoint) |
| **Google Forms** | Bug reporting | **Only what you voluntarily submit** |
| **Abacus API** | Download counting | None (only increments a counter) |
| **Tailscale (optional)** | Server connectivity | None (user-installed, user-configured) |

**We do not share your data with these services.** Any data sent to them is either:
- Publicly available (GitHub)
- Voluntarily submitted by you (Google Forms)
- Anonymous aggregate data (Abacus API)

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Lumière is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

If you are under 13, please do not use Lumière.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at:

- `https://github.com/forge/lumiere/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`
- Or directly in the "Terms & Privacy" section of the application.

---

## 10. Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to delete, correct, or export.

If you have concerns, you can:
- Open an issue on GitHub
- Review the source code to verify our claims

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
